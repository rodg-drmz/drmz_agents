# Universal Design for Learning (UDL) Guidelines

## Overview
Universal Design for Learning is a framework to improve and optimize teaching and learning for all people based on scientific insights into how humans learn.

## Three Principles

### 1. Multiple Means of Representation
Provide information in multiple formats:
- Visual and auditory materials
- Text alternatives for images
- Captions for videos
- Multiple language options
- Clear, simple language

### 2. Multiple Means of Engagement
Stimulate interest and motivation:
- Offer choices in content and tools
- Provide relevant, authentic activities
- Minimize threats and distractions
- Foster collaboration and community
- Support self-regulation

### 3. Multiple Means of Action and Expression
Provide multiple ways for students to demonstrate learning:
- Flexible assessment methods
- Multiple response options
- Assistive technologies
- Graduated levels of support
- Practice with feedback

## Syllabus Checklist

- [ ] Multiple formats for course materials
- [ ] Clear accessibility statement
- [ ] Flexible assessment options
- [ ] Multiple ways to engage with content
- [ ] Support resources clearly listed
- [ ] Accommodations process explained
