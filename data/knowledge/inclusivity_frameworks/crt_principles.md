# Culturally Responsive Teaching (CRT) Principles

## Overview
Culturally Responsive Teaching is a pedagogy that recognizes the importance of including students' cultural references in all aspects of learning.

## Key Principles

### 1. Cultural Competence
- Acknowledge and respect diverse cultural backgrounds
- Incorporate diverse perspectives in course content
- Use culturally relevant examples and materials
- Recognize cultural differences in learning styles

### 2. Student-Centered Instruction
- Build on students' prior knowledge and experiences
- Create connections between course content and students' lives
- Encourage student voice and choice
- Foster collaborative learning environments

### 3. Critical Consciousness
- Encourage critical thinking about social issues
- Address power dynamics and social justice
- Promote awareness of diverse perspectives
- Support students in becoming agents of change

## Syllabus Checklist

- [ ] Welcoming, inclusive language
- [ ] Diverse perspectives in readings/materials
- [ ] Culturally relevant examples
- [ ] Recognition of diverse learning styles
- [ ] Support for first-generation students
- [ ] International student considerations
- [ ] Cultural sensitivity in assignments
