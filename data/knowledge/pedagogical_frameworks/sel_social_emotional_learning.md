# Social Emotional Learning (SEL) Framework

## Overview
Social Emotional Learning is the process through which children and adults acquire and effectively apply the knowledge, attitudes, and skills necessary to understand and manage emotions, set and achieve positive goals, feel and show empathy for others, establish and maintain positive relationships, and make responsible decisions.

## CASEL Framework - Five Core Competencies

### 1. Self-Awareness
The ability to accurately recognize one's own emotions, thoughts, and values and how they influence behavior.

**Skills Include:**
- Identifying emotions
- Accurate self-perception
- Recognizing strengths and needs
- Self-confidence
- Self-efficacy

**Examples in Syllabus:**
- Reflection activities on learning styles
- Self-assessment opportunities
- Growth mindset language
- Recognition of individual strengths

### 2. Self-Management
The ability to successfully regulate one's emotions, thoughts, and behaviors in different situations.

**Skills Include:**
- Impulse control
- Stress management
- Self-discipline
- Self-motivation
- Goal setting
- Organizational skills

**Examples in Syllabus:**
- Clear deadlines and time management support
- Stress reduction resources
- Study strategies and organization tips
- Goal-setting activities
- Self-regulation strategies

### 3. Social Awareness
The ability to take the perspective of and empathize with others, including those from diverse backgrounds and cultures.

**Skills Include:**
- Perspective-taking
- Empathy
- Appreciating diversity
- Respect for others
- Understanding social norms

**Examples in Syllabus:**
- Diverse perspectives in readings
- Cultural sensitivity in examples
- Community engagement opportunities
- Discussion of multiple viewpoints
- Respectful dialogue expectations

### 4. Relationship Skills
The ability to establish and maintain healthy and rewarding relationships with diverse individuals and groups.

**Skills Include:**
- Communication
- Social engagement
- Relationship building
- Teamwork
- Conflict resolution

**Examples in Syllabus:**
- Collaborative projects
- Peer review processes
- Group work guidelines
- Communication expectations
- Conflict resolution strategies

### 5. Responsible Decision-Making
The ability to make constructive choices about personal behavior and social interactions based on ethical standards, safety concerns, and social norms.

**Skills Include:**
- Identifying problems
- Analyzing situations
- Solving problems
- Evaluating
- Reflecting
- Ethical responsibility

**Examples in Syllabus:**
- Ethical guidelines for assignments
- Academic integrity policies
- Decision-making frameworks
- Problem-solving activities
- Reflection on choices

## Benefits

- Improved academic performance
- Better social relationships
- Reduced emotional distress
- Enhanced mental health
- Increased empathy and perspective-taking

- Preparation for life and work

## Evidence Base

Research shows SEL programs:
- Improve academic achievement by 11 percentile points
- Increase prosocial behaviors
- Reduce emotional distress
- Improve attitudes toward school
- Enhance social and emotional skills
