🌌 Project: DRMZ — Dreamz for a Decentralized Future
DRMZ Web3 & DRMZ Academy is a forward-thinking venture at the intersection of blockchain, AI, and emerging technologies, dedicated to revolutionizing education and civic engagement.

We operate as both a Cardano stake pool and a Web3 learning collective, blending impactful educational content, innovative products, and immersive events to help individuals and communities understand, apply, and benefit from the decentralized web.

Our work spans:

Cardano Stake Pool Operations (ticker: DRMZ) — securing the network while offering delegators meaningful returns and governance influence.

Web3 & AI Education — gamified learning, hands-on workshops, and agentic AI tools that make complex ideas accessible and actionable.

Community-Building Events — meetups, hackathons, and conferences that inspire, connect, and empower.

Our mission is to educate, empower, and connect people while promoting the ethical and positive use of technology. We are committed to making decentralized literacy, AI fluency, and blockchain participation accessible to all — and while operating as a for-profit business, we reinvest in charitable activities and community initiatives that align with our vision.

DRMZ is:
"Securing and Decentralizing Cardano"
"Facilitating Critical Conversations"
"Powering Web3 Literacy & Education"

"DRMZ is where learning meets innovation, staking meets empowerment, and community meets the future."

🧑‍🏫 About Rod (@rodg_drmz)
Rodrigo Gomez — also known as Rod — is a co-founder of DRMZ, professor of English, curriculum designer, and Emerging Technologies Specialist at San Diego Miramar College. As a Cardano Ambassador, he leads:

Development of Morpheus, the DRMZ AI Agent

AI and blockchain curriculum design for SDCCD and beyond

Large-scale governance literacy workshops and roleplay simulations

Educational toolkits for syllabus review, AI literacy, and staking onboarding

Cross-chain educational collaborations and institutional partnerships

Rod bridges institutions and innovations, code and classroom, dreamers and developers.
