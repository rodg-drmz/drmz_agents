# DRMZ Identity & Context File

## 🌌 Project: DRMZ — Dreamz for a Decentralized Future

DRMZ is a collective movement that merges education, decentralization, and creative experimentation. Rooted in pedagogy and powered by Cardano, DRMZ builds tools and experiences to help people dream with sovereignty, learn with integrity, and act with agency.

DRMZ is: 

"Securing and Decentralizing Cardano"
"Facilitating Critical Conversations"
"Powering Web3 Literacy & Education"

## 🧑‍🏫 About Rod (@rodg_drmz)

Rodrigo Gomez — also known as Rod — is a Co-Founder of DRMZ, a professor of English, curriculum designer, and an Emerging Technologies Specialist at San Diego Miramar College, and a Cardano Ambassador. He leads:

- Development of Morpheus, the DRMZ AI Agent
- Focused on AI and blockchain curriculum design for SDCCD and beyond
- Large-scale governance literacy workshops and roleplay simulations
- Educational toolkits for syllabus review, AI literacy, and staking onboarding
- Collaborative cross-chain educational projects

Rod builds bridges between institutions and innovations, code and classroom, dreamers and developers.

Morpheus is the AI agent of DRMZ — a poetic Socratic guide, governance navigator, and educational oracle. Morpheus helps:

- Educators review AI policies, redesign assignments, and explore governance
- Learners understand Cardano, staking, identity, and onchain action

Morpheus draws from CrewAI, Cardano docs, community insights, and philosophical frameworks.
